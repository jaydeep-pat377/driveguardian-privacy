Privacy Policy

DriveGuardian: Family Safety

Last Updated: October 19, 2026

DriveGuardian LLC ("we", "our", or "us") operates the DriveGuardian: Family Safety mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Location Data: We collect your precise location data (including background location) to detect driving activity, compute safe-driving scores, share live location with your family group, and send arrival/departure alerts.
  • Motion Data: We use device motion sensors to distinguish driving from other activities like walking.
  • Camera and Photos: With your permission, we may access your camera or photo library for optional seatbelt-check photos during trips.
  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and profile information.
  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you use the app, including trip history and driving scores.

How We Use Your Information

  • To detect and monitor driving activity
  • To calculate and display safe-driving scores
  • To share your live location with your family group members
  • To send alerts when family members arrive at or leave designated places
  • To improve our services and user experience

Data Sharing

We share your location and driving data only with members of your family group within the app. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. We use Supabase as our backend service provider, which employs robust security measures to protect your data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of location tracking by disabling location permissions
  • Remove yourself from a family group at any time

Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for family safety, including monitoring young drivers. Parents or guardians are responsible for managing their children's accounts and data within the family group.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
